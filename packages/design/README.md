# @nlpf/design

The Canal Light design system shared by the dashboard and the project site.

- `tokens.css`: every colour, font family, size, radius, shadow, space and easing. Nothing else in the repository writes a colour literal.
- `fonts.css` and `fonts/`: Instrument Serif, Instrument Sans and JetBrains Mono, self-hosted Latin subsets under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- `logo/`: the one-window mark. `mark.svg` is one colour with a knocked-out window; `mark-color.svg` and `mark-color-dark.svg` light the window in oranje; `favicon.svg` follows the browser theme; `app-icon.svg` is the square icon.

The direction and its reasoning are in `docs/design/canal-light.md` and `docs/design/site-3d.md`.
