Beschrijving

Exclusive penthouse with spacious balcony of 13m², located on the ninth floor of the prestigious new construction project Residential Building De Hofdame, completed in 2007.

Top location
Living in a popular, cozy spot in the center of Rotterdam!
Due to the central location with almost all major urban amenities within walking distance (hip restaurants and trendy bars, small boutiques, public transport, cinemas, theater, museums, etc.) about eight minutes by car from the Rotterdam Ring.
The complex features a beautiful inner garden.

CENTRAL ENTRANCE
The entrance of the apartment complex is spacious. The communal inner garden is the core of De Hofdame. The dimensions of 1800 m2 are unique for the city center of Rotterdam. The garden architect of De Hofdame was inspired by Renzo Piano’s garden on Rue de Meaux, Paris, for the choice of birch trees.

Ground floor:
Closed central entrance with doorbell panel, video intercom, mailboxes, access to 5 staircases and elevators, and the beautiful inner garden. There are also 2 communal bicycle storages.

PENTHOUSE
9th floor:
The residence is centrally located next to the historic Laurenskerk with a free, spectacular view over the Rotterdam skyline.

An impression:

  • Entrance hall with wardrobe, tiled toilet with fountain, and meter cupboard.
  • Very spacious and bright living room with air conditioning, access via sliding door to the spacious, sunny balcony with full-width awning.
  • Dining room also with access to the balcony.
  • Half open, modern, L-shaped kitchen equipped with Siemens built-in appliances (induction hob, extractor hood, combination microwave oven, dishwasher, and refrigerator).
  • Indoor storage room with washing machine, dryer, and freezer.
  • First bedroom with air conditioning and French balconies.
  • Second bedroom (also study/home office) with French balcony.
  • Closet room (can also be used as a third bedroom).
  • Luxury bathroom with double sink, bathtub, and shower.
    Striking are the large windows throughout the entire residence.
    Also due to the location on the ninth floor, the impressive view, the cozy and warm interior and finish of the entire residence, the apartment is a sought-after place.

Additional Costs:

PARKING
Indoor closed private parking garage on the first floor. The rental cost of the parking space is €150,- per month.
